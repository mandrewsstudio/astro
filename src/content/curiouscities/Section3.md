---
title: Reshape the streets
---
During a week of disconcerting predictions and scary statistics, the Curious Cities Assembly was a much needed injection of playfulness and pragmatism. The look and feel was a massive hit with attendees, with t-shirts, notebooks and posters flying off the merch table.

This identity helped the event have a massive impact, creating a space in which policymakers, urban planners and environmental advocates could feel comfortable and energised to make real changes to the way our cities are designed.
