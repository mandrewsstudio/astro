---
title: The pavement is a playground
---
 Imagine what a city could look like if it was built for play. Not just swings and roundabouts, but spaces for curiosity, intrigue, art, expression and conversation. We created the brand identity, name and messaging in the spirit of child-like curiosity.

 At the core of the brand is the idea of the pavement as a playground, with a library of colourful, hand-cut paper shapes, conjuring a vibrant space that can be interacted with and rearranged by its participants.
